About Sweet Crumb Bakery

Sweet Crumb Bakery is a fictional local bakery created for our Front-End Development project.

Our concept focuses on fresh products, warm design and a welcoming atmosphere for customers.

Sweet Crumb Bakery started as a small family idea. We carefully select ingredients, bake every morning, and create seasonal desserts that bring people together. Our team loves experimenting with new flavours while keeping traditional recipes alive.
